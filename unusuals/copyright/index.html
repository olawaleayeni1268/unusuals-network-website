1. Defined Terms

This policy concerns content made available on Unusu@ls, a social product of Unusuals Network.

2. Submission Type

Ordinary content reports: If you are not a rights holder or their authorised agent, please use appropriate app reporting tools when available or contact safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. This is distinct from a formal intellectual-property complaint.

Copyright complaints: Email legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng with your contact details, the original work, the exact link to the content, your good-faith basis for claiming infringement, your authority to act and your signature. We may request verification.

Trade mark complaints: Identify the trade mark or registration, the challenged account/content, the risk of confusion or misuse, ownership or authority, and a safe contact route.

Submitting a complaint: Use the legal contact above. Do not include passwords, account tokens or unrelated personal data.

3. Counter-Notification and Appeals Process

If material is removed after a rights complaint, the affected user may request reconsideration by contacting legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng with the referenced decision, their account handle, content identification, a reasoned explanation and supporting evidence.

Requests for reconsideration are assessed according to applicable law and the operator responsible for the affected material. Unusu@ls cannot guarantee removal of copies hosted by others.

4. Repeat Infringer and Abusive Reporting Policy

Repeated or serious infringements can lead to action under applicable law and service rules. Misleading or abusive complaints may also be reviewed.

5. Transparency and Compliance

We review requests sent through our legal contact according to the information provided and applicable law. Independently hosted copies are controlled by their service operators.

6. Use of Your Copyrighted or Trademarked Content

Creators continue to own rights they hold in their submissions. The limited licence described in our Terms supports service operation and relevant moderation and communication. Unauthorised use of someone else's content or brand may breach these policies.

AT Protocol interoperability can distribute public material to independent services. Our ability to remove third-party copies is limited by those services' control.

7. Contact

Formal intellectual-property notices: legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. Related safety matters: safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. General support: support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, private keys or unnecessary sensitive documents.
