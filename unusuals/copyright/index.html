1. Defined Terms

This draft is for Unusu@ls, operated by Unusuals Network. “Content” refers to material made available through the product; “rights holder” refers to a person or organisation owning a relevant copyright, trade mark or other intellectual-property right, or an authorised representative.

2. Submission Type

Ordinary content reports: If you are not a rights holder or their authorised agent, please use appropriate app reporting tools when available or contact safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. This is distinct from a formal intellectual-property complaint.

Copyright complaints: A rights holder or authorised representative may email legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng identifying the original work, relevant post or record, rights claimed, contact information, reason permission is lacking and their authority to act. We may request additional verification.

Trade mark complaints: Identify the trade mark or registration, the challenged account/content, the risk of confusion or misuse, ownership or authority, and a safe contact route.

Not yet implemented: Dedicated complaint submission forms are not claimed to exist. The legal intake and supporting evidence-handling procedures require testing and review.

3. Counter-Notification and Appeals Process

If material is removed after a rights complaint, the affected user may request reconsideration by contacting legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng with the referenced decision, their account handle, content identification, a reasoned explanation and supporting evidence.

Whether a formal statutory counter-notice mechanism or additional jurisdictional declarations apply must be determined by qualified legal review. No US-specific oath, court consent, statutory timeline or dedicated form is adopted by default.

4. Repeat Infringer and Abusive Reporting Policy

Repeated or serious infringement may justify appropriate action under our Terms and Community Guidelines. Knowingly false, harassing or abusive rights reports may also warrant action. The thresholds, human-review responsibilities and evidence retention must be settled in the operating runbook.

5. Transparency and Compliance

We intend to process relevant notices consistently with applicable law and protect necessary evidence. Any public transparency report, removal metrics, EU Digital Services Act procedure or government reporting arrangement must be supported by an actual operational capability and legal determination before it is claimed.

6. Use of Your Copyrighted or Trademarked Content

Creators continue to own rights they hold in their submissions. The limited licence proposed in our Terms supports service operation and relevant moderation and communication. Unauthorised use of someone else's content or brand may breach these policies.

AT Protocol interoperability can distribute public material to independent services. Our ability to remove third-party copies is limited by those services' control.

7. Contact

Formal intellectual-property notices: legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. Related safety matters: safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. General support: support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, private keys or unnecessary sensitive documents.
