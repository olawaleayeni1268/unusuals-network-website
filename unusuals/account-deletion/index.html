Request account deletion

Unusu@ls users must be able to request deletion without reinstalling the app. The external request route proposed in the existing operating draft is email to:

privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng

Use the email subject: UNUSU@LS ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST.

Include only your account handle or username, the email address associated with the account, a statement that you request account and associated-data deletion, and a safe contact route for verification.

Never include a password, one-time code, recovery code, private key or CAPTCHA response.

What happens after a request?

  1. Request received: our designated team receives your email through the privacy contact route.
  2. Identity verification: we may ask you to confirm control of the account or registered email. This helps prevent malicious deletion.
  3. Review and execution: after verification, Unusuals Network initiates deletion through the supported account and data-removal processes.
  4. Completion information: we provide the expected completion path after verification. No fixed completion-time promise is made until the handling runbook is approved.

The exact end-to-end deletion process remains a release gate; this preview does not claim deletion operations are certified.

What may be deleted?

Subject to the verified deletion pathway, the intended scope includes account and authentication records; profile details; posts, replies and social records stored on systems we control; associated media and blobs; active sessions; notification tokens and unnecessary service or support-linked data.

The final production test must confirm which records and linked systems are actually covered.

What information may be kept?

We may retain limited information where required for law, security, child safety, fraud prevention, disputes, evidence of compliance or backup rotation.

The source V1 draft proposes, subject to review, up to 24 months for deletion-request/completion records and normally up to 90 days for certain security logs and rotating backups. These timings require policy/legal and operational sign-off.

A note about AT Protocol federation

Public content may already have been copied, cached or indexed by independently operated AT Protocol services. We cannot guarantee that independently held copies disappear immediately or completely after an account is deleted from Unusuals Network-operated systems.

Is deletion reversible?

Deletion may be irreversible. Save or export any information you wish to retain before confirming deletion. Your account may need additional verification before the request can proceed.

In-app deletion

An in-app deletion option is also required and must be tested in the exact accepted Android release. Availability is not asserted here until that test passes.

Need assistance?

Contact privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for deletion or data-rights questions, support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for general support, or legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for legal notices.
