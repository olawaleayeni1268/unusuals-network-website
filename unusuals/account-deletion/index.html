About Unusu@ls

Unusuals Network operates Unusu@ls, a social-networking product based on the AT Protocol.

Request account deletion

To request deletion of an account hosted by Unusu@ls without using the app, send an email from your account's registered address to:

privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng

Use the email subject Account deletion request and include your Unusu@ls handle.

Send your account handle and the linked email address. Our privacy contact may request evidence that the account belongs to you before processing deletion.

Never include a password, one-time code, recovery code, private key or CAPTCHA response.

What happens after a request?

  1. Request received: our designated team receives your email through the privacy contact route.
  2. Identity verification: we may ask you to confirm control of the account or registered email. This helps prevent malicious deletion.
  3. Processing: verified requests for Unusu@ls-hosted accounts are processed through our account server. If your account is hosted elsewhere, such as bsky.social, ask that provider to delete it.
  4. Status: reply to your original privacy email to request an update. No fixed time to completion is promised.

Deletion can be irreversible. Save data that you wish to keep before asking for removal.

What may be deleted?

For accounts hosted on our server, deletion concerns account and repository information controlled by that server. Independently operated services may hold separate copies and their own records.

Public AT Protocol records can be copied or indexed by independent relays, services and apps beyond our direct control.

What information may be kept?

We may retain limited information where required for law, security, child safety, fraud prevention, disputes, evidence of compliance or backup rotation.

The specific retention durations remain subject to approval before this page is published.

A note about AT Protocol federation

Public content may already have been copied, cached or indexed by independently operated AT Protocol services. We cannot guarantee that independently held copies disappear immediately or completely after an account is deleted from Unusuals Network-operated systems.

Is deletion reversible?

Deletion may be irreversible. Save or export any information you wish to retain before confirming deletion. Your account may need additional verification before the request can proceed.

In-app deletion

In the app open Settings → Account → Delete account, request an email verification code and enter it with your password. Completion requires the account host and associated services to respond.

Need assistance?

Contact privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for deletion or data-rights questions, support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for general support, or legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for legal notices.
