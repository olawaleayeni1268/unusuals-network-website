Before this page goes live

Content is a faithful review adaptation of our existing Repo 4 publication candidate. Exact product behaviour, operating times and legal terms require owner and relevant professional approval.

Contact our support team

For account access, sign-in problems, posting, media, general questions or Google Play review assistance, contact:

support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng

Official role-based inbound addresses were externally tested in August 2026. We must recheck live delivery and establish response ownership before publication. Replies may initially come from an authorised Unusuals Network Gmail address while custom-domain outbound messaging is pending.

Use the right contact route

  • Account access, product questions and troubleshooting: support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng
  • Privacy, data rights and account deletion: privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng
  • Safety, harassment and child-safety concerns: safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng
  • Formal legal and intellectual-property notices: legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng

In an immediate emergency or threat to life, contact emergency services or the appropriate authority first.

What to include in your message

It helps to include your account handle, app version, Android version, a short problem description, steps that reproduce it, approximate time, and a redacted screenshot if necessary.

Never send passwords, OTPs, private keys, CAPTCHA tokens, national identity numbers or financial credentials.

Account deletion

You can request account deletion without reinstalling Unusu@ls. Review the Account Deletion page to understand the email request route, identity checks and important AT Protocol federation limitations.

Reporting harmful content

For safety concerns, use in-app report tools where available in the accepted release. Send urgent concerns to safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. Do not attach or redistribute suspected illegal child-abuse material.

Information for Google Play reviewers

Reviewer test credentials and special navigation steps are supplied privately through the appropriate Play Console workflow after acceptance testing. They will never be published on this page.

Availability and service standard

A formal support response-time target and escalation owner have not yet been approved. This draft deliberately makes no unverified promise about response times.

Review status

This is a non-public design preview. Final policies require approval, a confirmed effective date, verified feature behaviour, tested email routing and the launch acceptance gates. Return to Trust & Support Centre.
