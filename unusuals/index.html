Unusu@ls

A Nigerian-born social network for conversation, discovery and community.

Express

Share what matters

Unusu@ls is designed for public conversation through text, images, video and social interactions.

Discover

Find people and perspectives

Explore creators, interests, culture, useful conversations and communities beyond the people you already know.

Connect

Built on an open social foundation

Unusu@ls uses the AT Protocol, an open social-networking foundation that supports portable identity and connected social experiences.

Product status

Unusu@ls is under active development and controlled testing. Unusuals Network is completing launch-readiness work across reliability, safety, account recovery, support, moderation and release governance before broader public availability.

This corporate page describes the product and its operator. It is not a claim that every planned capability is publicly available today.
