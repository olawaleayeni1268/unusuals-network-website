1. Agreement and eligibility

These Terms govern access to Unusu@ls, a social-networking product operated by Unusuals Network. They should be read alongside the app-specific Privacy Policy and Community Guidelines.

Users must be at least 13, and must also meet any higher age or authorisation requirement applicable where they live. Account information must be accurate; users must not open accounts for others without authority.

2. Account responsibilities

Protect your login credentials and report suspected compromise to support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. Selling accounts, impersonating others, evading restrictions or automating account creation without authorisation is prohibited.

3. Your content and licence

You retain ownership of content you create, subject to the rights you grant to operate the service. The original draft proposes a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence for Unusuals Network to host, store, reproduce, process, transmit, display and distribute submitted content as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, moderate, promote and improve Unusu@ls and compatible AT Protocol services.

The licence ends when content is deleted from systems we control, subject to reasonable exceptions for backups, security, legal duties, disputes, moderation evidence and copies already distributed through independently operated services. You must have the necessary rights to anything you post.

4. Rules for participating

Users must not harass, threaten, bully, stalk, impersonate, defraud or expose private information without authority. We also prohibit child sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, serious violence, hateful abuse, spam, scams, malicious automation, malware, illegal activity and intellectual-property infringement.

Read the fuller Community Guidelines and Safety Standards.

5. Moderation, reports and appeals

Depending on context and risk, actions may include warnings, content labels, restricted visibility, removal, feature restrictions, suspension, termination, preservation of evidence and lawful escalation. Severe risks may require immediate action before notice.

Use available in-app reporting or blocking tools where supported by the accepted release. You may write to safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng about urgent safety concerns and appeals, or support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for general support. The specific reporting and appeal processes must be validated on the release candidate.

6. Copyright and rights complaints

Rights holders or authorised representatives may write to legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng with the material or record URL, the claimed right, an explanation of the complaint and contact information. We may request supporting evidence and take action consistent with applicable law.

7. Changes and availability

Unusu@ls is under development and controlled testing. Features may be changed, limited or unavailable. We do not guarantee continuous service, permanent storage, a particular ranking or audience, or any specific creator revenue or outcome.

8. Open protocol and third parties

Public AT Protocol records may be distributed to and cached by other operators. We do not control all independent services and cannot promise that they will immediately remove copies after you delete your content or account.

9. Suspending or deleting accounts

Users may stop using the service. Our account-deletion page explains the external request route and deletion considerations; an operational in-app deletion process must also be independently verified. Deleted accounts and content may not be recoverable. Lawfully necessary records may be retained for security, safety, legal duties, disputes and backup rotation.

We may suspend or terminate access for serious violations or other lawful reasons, subject to appropriate processes.

10. Disclaimers and liability

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the service is provided on an “as available” basis. We cannot guarantee that all user-generated content is accurate, safe, lawful or suitable. Users should not rely on it as a substitute for professional or emergency advice.

The source draft proposes limitations on indirect and consequential losses and user responsibility for unlawful misuse only to the extent permitted by law. Nothing may exclude a right or liability that applicable law makes non-excludable. The exact legal wording requires qualified review before publication.

11. Governing law and disputes

The draft proposes the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, while recognising mandatory rights that may apply elsewhere. Contact legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng to raise legal concerns or attempt good-faith resolution; urgent legal remedies remain available where applicable.
