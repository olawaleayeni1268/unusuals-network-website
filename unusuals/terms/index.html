4. Moderation and Illegal Content

Our rules prohibit child exploitation, credible threats, violence, scams, non-consensual intimate images, harassment and other unlawful content. For content and accounts hosted on our server, we may remove material or suspend accounts when justified.

App reports are directed to a supported moderation service chosen in the report form; certain serious categories are restricted to Bluesky's moderation service. Safety concerns relating to Unusu@ls can also be emailed to safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng.
