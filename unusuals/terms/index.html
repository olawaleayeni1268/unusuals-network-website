1. Eligibility and Age Verification

Unusu@ls is intended for people aged 13 or older. Where a higher legal minimum or a valid parental authorisation is required, that requirement governs access. We may need to restrict access where eligibility cannot be confirmed; the accepted release's age checks and any additional assurance measures must be verified before publication.

2. Account Registration and Responsibilities

Each user is responsible for accurate registration information, protection of credentials and activity carried out through their account. Do not impersonate another person, trade accounts, evade restrictions or use automated signups without authorisation.

Report suspected account compromise promptly to support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. If acting for a business or organisation, you must have authority to represent it.

3. User Content

Your posts, profile information, images, videos, replies, links and other material you submit are your responsibility. You must own or have permission to publish and license that material. You retain ownership of your content.

To operate the service, the existing draft proposes a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence allowing Unusuals Network to host, reproduce, format, transmit, display, distribute and process content for service delivery, safety, moderation and promotion. The legal scope, sublicensing and post-deletion treatment require final review.

Unusu@ls uses AT Protocol-compatible public records. Other operators may receive, copy, index or store public material. We cannot promise that deleting content from our systems will erase independent copies elsewhere. See Privacy and Account Deletion.

4. Proactive Moderation and Illegal Content

Our rules prohibit child exploitation, credible threats, serious violence, scams, non-consensual intimate images, harassment, extremist activity and other unlawful or seriously harmful conduct. We may investigate reports, restrict visibility, remove material or suspend accounts when justified by context and risk.

Bluesky's reference framework describes specific automated detection, hash-matching, human moderation and institutional reporting partnerships. Those capabilities are not yet independently verified for Unusu@ls and are not promised by this draft. A production moderation audit must establish our exact tools, escalation authority and response procedures.

For urgent safety concerns: safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng.

5. Discover Feed and Recommender Systems

Unusu@ls provides content discovery through interests, feeds and recommendation-related features. Feed ordering may depend on user selections, public content, interaction signals and compatible services. Federated or third-party feeds may apply rules that Unusuals Network does not control.

Review required: document which recommendations actually operate in the approved Android release, available user controls and any opt-out mechanism. Do not assume Bluesky's Discover Feed settings are present in Unusu@ls.

6. Complaints and Appeals

Users may flag content and accounts through available in-app reporting options where supported. Safety concerns may also be emailed to safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng with the account identifier, post URL, issue and any relevant context. Do not email suspected illegal child-abuse material.

If a moderation action affects your account or content, you may request review with sufficient identifying details and an explanation. The exact in-app appeals process, notification mechanisms, time limits and review standards require operational validation. No Bluesky appeal deadline or EU-specific procedure is adopted automatically.

7. Termination

Users may stop using Unusu@ls. Account deletion may be requested through our external deletion route; in-app deletion must be independently validated for the final release. Certain safety, legal, audit and backup records may remain as permitted by law and the approved retention policy.

We may restrict, suspend or terminate accounts in response to severe policy breaches, legal orders or risks to others, applying appropriate review where practicable. Federation may mean some content persists on independent services.

8. Third-Party Services

Links, externally hosted feeds, third-party applications and other AT Protocol services may have their own conditions and privacy practices. Their inclusion does not automatically mean they are owned or endorsed by Unusuals Network.

Check a third party's rules before disclosing information or relying on its services. We cannot control how an independent operator processes information that you share with it.

9. Open Source Software

The app and supporting services use open-source components and the AT Protocol ecosystem. Individual components remain governed by their applicable open-source licences. A release-specific third-party notices and attribution audit is required to confirm accurate licence statements before publication.

10. Warranty Disclaimer

Unusu@ls is under active development and controlled testing. Features and content may change, be temporarily unavailable or contain errors. We cannot guarantee uninterrupted operation, any particular content ranking or that user-generated information will always be accurate or safe.

Any disclaimers must be interpreted only to the extent permitted by applicable law, without excluding mandatory rights or duties.

11. Indemnification

The existing Unusu@ls draft contemplates user responsibility for claims or losses caused by unlawful content, infringement or serious misuse of the service. The scope and enforceability of any indemnification obligation require qualified legal review under Nigerian and other applicable law; this preview does not introduce new binding obligations.

12. Limitation of Liability

The original Unusu@ls draft proposes limiting certain indirect or consequential damages only where the law permits. We will not copy Bluesky's fixed monetary cap, legal exceptions or liability allocation without independent legal review. Nothing here is intended to exclude rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.

13. Governing Law, Jurisdiction, Venue, Dispute Resolution, and Arbitration

Our existing publication candidate identifies the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, subject to mandatory laws and rights that apply elsewhere. Disputes may first be raised with legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for good-faith discussion.

Bluesky's Delaware governing-law terms, US arbitration institutions and collective-action provisions are reference topics, not adopted contractual provisions for Unusu@ls. Any arbitration, venue or dispute-resolution wording requires qualified legal approval.

14. Updates to Terms

We may revise these Terms as the app and legal requirements evolve. Material revisions should be communicated in an appropriate way and reflected by a clearly displayed effective date. The effective date for this draft has not yet been approved.
