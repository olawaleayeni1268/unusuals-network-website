About Unusu@ls

Unusuals Network operates Unusu@ls, a social-networking product based on the AT Protocol.

1. Safety First

Public safety and serious harm: No use of Unusu@ls to coordinate violent crime, terrorism, exploitation, trafficking, credible threats or other acts of serious harm. Documentary, artistic and educational contexts require proportional assessment, not automatic equivalence with endorsement.

Children: Child sexual exploitation, grooming, coercion, solicitation or abusive material are prohibited. Do not redistribute illegal material or attach it to email reports. Send the account or post link and a description to safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng; seek emergency assistance where someone faces immediate danger.

Content restrictions, safety labels and age-related settings vary by app version and the services involved.

Privacy and animal welfare: Doxxing, stolen credentials and malicious disclosure of private records are prohibited; content deliberately encouraging cruelty or serious abuse of animals is also unacceptable.

2. Respect Others

We prohibit hate, dehumanising targeting and threats against individuals or groups because of protected characteristics. Harassment includes persistent targeting, stalking, unwanted sexual advances, coordinated campaigns to intimidate or humiliate, and misuse of report mechanisms.

A report's context, risk and applicable legal protections should be considered by the service reviewing it.

3. Be Authentic

No phishing, fabricated engagement, spam, investment fraud, deceptive fundraising or impersonation intended to mislead others. Do not sell or transfer accounts deceptively or falsely claim official verification.

Satire, parody and clearly identified fictional or fan accounts may be legitimate when they avoid harmful deception. Harmful manipulated media and deliberately deceptive civic information can warrant restriction under applicable rules.

4. Follow the Rules

Do not use the platform to facilitate unlawful trade in prohibited or restricted products, exploit others, infringe copyright or trade marks, share malware, compromise systems or evade enforced restrictions.

Respect creators' rights. See Copyright & Intellectual Property.

5. Protected Expression

These rules are intended to allow public-interest reporting, education, independent journalism, criticism, advocacy, satire, artistic work, anti-violence messaging and support for people recovering from harm.

Discussion of serious events, suicide prevention, public records, health and recovery may be permitted when handled responsibly without encouraging violence, exploitation, harassment or illegal activity. A legitimate public-interest purpose does not remove applicable privacy or safety duties.

6. Reporting, Enforcement and Appeals

In the app, use the report form to choose a moderation service. Reports in certain serious categories go to Bluesky's moderation service. For urgent concerns relating to Unusu@ls, email safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng; include URLs, not passwords or illegal media.

The moderation service receiving a report determines its enforcement and review process. Unusu@ls cannot guarantee outcomes for independently operated moderation services.
