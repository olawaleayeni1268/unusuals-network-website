Our commitment

Unusu@ls is intended for expression, conversation and community—not abuse or exploitation. These Guidelines apply to posts, replies, public profiles, account names, images, videos, audio, links and other supported platform interactions.

1. Protect children

We prohibit child sexual abuse material, grooming, sexual exploitation, trafficking, coercion and attempts to obtain sexual content from a child. Report suspected abuse promptly to safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. Do not email or redistribute suspected illegal material—send the account or post link and a brief description instead.

For an immediate danger to life or safety, contact appropriate emergency or law-enforcement authorities first.

2. No threats, violence or extremist harm

Credible threats, incitement or facilitation of serious violence, and support for violent extremist organisations are prohibited. Context such as reporting, education, documentation and counterspeech may matter.

3. No hate, harassment or stalking

We prohibit dehumanising or threatening attacks based on protected characteristics, coordinated targeting, persistent harassment, stalking, unwanted sexual advances, and sharing personal information to endanger another person. Criticism of ideas and public conduct does not automatically constitute prohibited abuse.

4. No exploitation or non-consensual intimate content

Non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual extortion, voyeuristic sexual exploitation and any sexual content involving minors are prohibited. Other adult content may be restricted, labelled or removed according to law, age, context and actual product controls.

5. No scams, deceptive identities or malicious activity

Phishing, fraud, impersonation, credential theft, fabricated engagement, malicious automation, spam, malware, threats to service security and unlawful access attempts are prohibited. Clearly identified satire or parody must not be used to facilitate harmful deception.

6. Privacy, rights and dangerous activity

Users must not publish private information without authority, infringe intellectual-property rights or facilitate illegal goods, trafficking or seriously dangerous activity. Send rights complaints to legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng.

7. Harmful misinformation and self-harm

Unusu@ls may label, restrict or remove materially deceptive content that creates serious risk of harm, fraud, impersonation or public-safety damage. We distinguish satire, opinion, good-faith error and reporting from coordinated harmful deception.

Content encouraging, instructing or coordinating self-harm is prohibited. Recovery, supportive discussion and public-interest reporting may be allowed.

8. Reporting a concern

Where available in the released app, use the content or account's Report control. Alternatively email safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng with:

  • Account handle or a post/record link.
  • A short explanation of the concern and why it is urgent.
  • Relevant dates or context, without unnecessary personal data.

Knowingly malicious or abusive reports may also be subject to enforcement. Please do not send passwords, recovery codes, private keys or illegal material.

9. Enforcement decisions

Depending on seriousness and context, Unusuals Network may request more evidence, warn, label, reduce visibility, remove material, restrict features, suspend or terminate an account, or preserve and lawfully refer evidence. We consider severity, intent, repetition, harm, public interest and available evidence. Protective action may precede notice in urgent cases.

10. Appeals

When an appeal is offered, include the affected account identifier, the action being challenged and relevant context. Until any in-app appeals system is certified, direct enquiries to safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. A different reviewer should handle serious appeals where operationally practicable; reversal is not guaranteed.

11. Child-safety escalation

The proposed operating standard includes prompt triage, restricted evidence access, preservation of necessary records, escalation to a designated safety owner and reporting to competent authorities when legally required. Response ownership, response targets and enforcement systems must be validated before public beta.
