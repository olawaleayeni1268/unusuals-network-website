Content is a faithful review adaptation of our existing Repo 4 publication candidate. Exact product behaviour, operating times and legal terms require owner and relevant professional approval.

1. About this policy

Unusuals Network operates Unusu@ls, an Android social-networking service built on the AT Protocol. This app-specific policy describes information we process when you use Unusu@ls, communicate with us or exercise privacy rights. It is distinct from the corporate website's privacy notice.

2. How to contact us

  • Privacy and data-rights requests: privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng
  • General support: support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng
  • Safety and abuse: safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng
  • Legal notices: legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng

Our role addresses route inbound messages to an owner-controlled inbox. A reply may come from an authorised Unusuals Network Gmail address while custom-domain outbound sending is under separate review.

3. Information we may process

Depending on the features actually used and enabled in the released application, this may include:

  • Account and eligibility data: email, username, handle, decentralised identifier (DID), authentication and recovery records, and age-related information.
  • Profile and public content: display name, biography, avatar, posts, replies, photographs, videos and audio contained in videos.
  • Social activity: follows, likes, reposts, saves, lists, feeds, searches, blocks, mutes and reports.
  • Device and service data: IP address, operating system and application version, notification tokens, security signals and technical logs.
  • Safety and support data: CAPTCHA signals, moderation records, appeals, support and deletion-request correspondence.
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance information, but only where enabled in the exact production configuration.

The final Android release and its enabled services must be checked before a definitive Google Play Data safety declaration is submitted. A library's technical capability does not automatically mean the service actively collects everything that library could access.

4. Why we process data

We use information as needed to create, authenticate, secure, recover and delete accounts; deliver profiles, posts, feeds, discovery, notifications and media; address safety and abuse; respond to requests; maintain service reliability; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Depending on the purpose and applicable law, lawful bases may include agreement performance, consent where required, legal compliance, vital interests and appropriately balanced legitimate interests.

5. Public posts and federation

Unusu@ls uses the AT Protocol. Public profiles, identifiers, posts and social records may be shared with, indexed or cached by independently operated services. Removing a record from systems under Unusuals Network's control does not ensure that copies previously received by third parties will disappear immediately or completely.

Avoid posting confidential information in public profiles or posts.

6. Service providers and transfers

Our current or proposed technology landscape includes Unusuals Network's account and media infrastructure; hosting, networking and security providers; Cloudflare Email Routing; Gmail routing for role-based correspondence; hCaptcha for abuse prevention; Google Play distribution; and independent AT Protocol services.

Other providers, such as diagnostic, transactional email or analytics services, must be included only if enabled in the release. Our final processor inventory, agreements and any relevant international-transfer assessment require pre-launch verification.

7. Retention and deletion

The source V1 draft proposes these normal retention periods, subject to legal and operational review:

  • Active account information: while needed to operate an account, subject to a valid deletion request and legal exceptions.
  • Posts, media and social records: until removed through account action, moderation or deletion, subject to independent federated copies.
  • Security and technical logs: normally up to 90 days, with exceptions for investigations, legal duties or system recovery.
  • Backups: normally up to 90 days as data ages out under backup rotation.
  • Support, privacy, legal, deletion-completion and relevant moderation records: normally up to 24 months after closure, subject to justified exceptions.

These periods are not certified operating guarantees until the approved runbooks, infrastructure and legal review confirm them.

8. Security

We aim to protect information using encrypted network connections, access controls, restricted handling of secrets, audit evidence and recovery procedures. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please safeguard your credentials and report suspected compromise.

9. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal or further information about processing. Write to privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng. We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request. You may also raise an applicable complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or another competent authority.

10. Account deletion

Our account-deletion guidance explains how to request deletion from outside the app and describes what may be removed or lawfully retained. The in-app process and full back-end deletion handling must be independently tested on the release candidate.

11. Age and child protection

Unusu@ls is not intended for children under 13. Where a higher age or parental-authorisation requirement applies, that requirement controls. Credible child-safety concerns may be reported to safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng; contact appropriate emergency services or authorities for immediate danger.

12. Ranking, recommendations and changes

Feeds and interactions may use signals to present content and discovery. Future high-impact behavioural profiling or recommendation programmes require separate assessment before deployment.

We may revise the policy as law, features and providers change. The published version must display an approved effective date, with material changes communicated appropriately.
