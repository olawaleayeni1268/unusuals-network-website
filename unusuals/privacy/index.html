1. Introduction

This notice explains our data handling in connection with Unusu@ls accounts, public feeds, posts, media, communications, security and support.

For data questions or requests, contact privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng.

2. What is Personal Data?

Personal data is information that can identify or be linked to a person directly or indirectly. Examples include email addresses, account handles, device identifiers, IP addresses and requests made to support. Information that has been genuinely anonymised may not have the same status.

3. Informing us of changes

Users should keep account contact information accurate and update available profile or recovery settings when they change. For a correction that the app cannot complete, contact our privacy team through the address below.

4. Applicability of this Policy

This notice covers processing by Unusuals Network in connection with Unusu@ls. Independent account hosts, AT Protocol applications and websites follow their own policies.

5. Third-party Services

Unusu@ls relies on services operated by Bluesky Social PBC for timelines, profiles, search and notifications, direct messages, video processing and push notifications. Depending on your activity, account identifiers, content requested or sent and push tokens can be processed by those services under Bluesky's privacy notice. We also use hCaptcha during sign-up to prevent abuse.

6. If you do not want to provide Personal Data

Account and recovery details are needed to register, secure and recover accounts. If required information is not supplied, some features will not work.

7. Updates to this Policy

Changes will be reflected in the version and effective date of the published policy. Important changes may be announced through the application, by email or through another appropriate channel, depending on available product capabilities and legal requirements.

8. Personal Data We Collect

Information associated with the functions you use can include:

  • Account and eligibility: email, handle, username, DID, account creation and recovery records, age-related information.
  • Public records: profiles, posts, replies, pictures, videos, follows, likes, reposts, lists and blocks. Direct messages and mutes are not public AT Protocol records.
  • Technical and safety: IP address, device/app versions, login and security logs, CAPTCHA signals and notification tokens where enabled.
  • Communications: correspondence about support, safety, privacy, legal requests and account deletion.
  • App use and region: some versions use usage events, feature settings and IP-derived regions with Bluesky-operated services; region helps apply age-related safety rules.

Posts, likes, follows, reposts and blocks are public AT Protocol records and may be copied by other services. Direct messages and mutes are not public AT Protocol records. We do not describe payment records or contact-book uploads as data collected by our own services here.

9. Cookies

Our websites and connected services may use essential browser storage for session management and security. The corporate website privacy notice separately covers the business website.

Some app versions transmit usage events and feature-configuration requests to Bluesky-operated endpoints. IP-based region settings help apply age-related requirements. These app requests are not necessarily browser cookies.

10. How We Use Personal Data

We use account and app information for sign-in, service delivery, feed discovery, account recovery, notification delivery, anti-abuse measures, support and applicable legal obligations.

Our processing is governed by applicable data-protection obligations, including those in Nigeria. Contact the privacy address below about your rights.

11. How We Share your Personal Data

Public AT Protocol records can be replicated by independently run relays, indexes and apps. Unusu@ls feeds index public post addresses, author identifiers and topic categories to select posts; they do not use private messages as feed inputs.

Other providers currently used for app functions include Bluesky Social PBC for app-view, messaging, videos, push notifications and some analytics and configuration services, alongside web hosting and anti-abuse providers such as Cloudflare and hCaptcha. Accounts created through our server are hosted by Unusu@ls.

12. International data transfers

Because the AT Protocol is federated and features rely on external providers, data may be processed outside Nigeria. Independent providers have their own data practices and notices.

13. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, portability and withdrawal of consent where relevant. Send a request to privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng and provide enough information for proportionate verification; never send a password or private key.

Independent federated copies may be beyond our direct control. You may also raise concerns with an appropriate data-protection regulator, including the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where applicable.

14. Retention of Personal Data

We may retain security, support, account and compliance records where lawful and necessary. Public posts already copied to independent AT Protocol systems may remain under those services' retention procedures.

For deletion requests and federation limitations, see Account Deletion. Contact privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng about records related to your account.

15. Supplemental Notice for Certain Jurisdictions

Privacy rights and rules differ by jurisdiction. Mandatory protections that apply to you continue to apply.

You may contact the relevant data-protection regulator where the law provides that right.

16. Security

We use appropriate security practices and access controls to protect account and support information. Online systems are not risk-free, so protect your credentials and report suspected compromise.

17. Children's Personal Information

Unusu@ls is not intended for children under 13 or below a higher age applicable in their region. For exploitation concerns, email safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng with URLs and descriptions, not illegal material.

18. Contact Us

Unusuals Network is responsible for this product's data-handling disclosures. Contact privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng about privacy rights or data questions; support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for account issues; safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for abuse; legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for legal matters.
