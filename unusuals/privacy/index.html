1. Introduction

Unusuals Network operates Unusu@ls, a social application built with AT Protocol-compatible services. This draft describes our potential personal-data processing for accounts, feeds, posts, media, communications, security and support. It is distinct from our corporate website's website privacy notice.

The final statement must reflect the exact approved app release and the Nigerian Data Protection Act and any other relevant laws.

2. What is Personal Data?

Personal data is information that can identify or be linked to a person directly or indirectly. Examples include email addresses, account handles, device identifiers, IP addresses and requests made to support. Information that has been genuinely anonymised may not have the same status.

3. Informing us of changes

Users should keep account contact information accurate and update available profile or recovery settings when they change. For a correction that the app cannot complete, contact our privacy team through the address below.

4. Applicability of this Policy

This draft applies to processing by Unusuals Network in connection with its Unusu@ls product. Independent AT Protocol applications, hosting operators and external sites may act under their own terms and privacy notices. This policy is not a universal policy for all AT Protocol participants.

5. Third-party Services

Some Unusu@ls features interact with other services, federated feeds or websites. These operators may collect information under their own policies. We must maintain a verified list of production processors and independent recipients; a technology mentioned in this preview does not prove that it collects data in the released app.

6. If you do not want to provide Personal Data

Some account, security and recovery information may be necessary to register or use particular app functions. If required information is not provided, we may be unable to supply that function. Optional data, where applicable, should have appropriately documented choices.

7. Updates to this Policy

Changes will be reflected in the version and effective date of the published policy. Important changes may be announced through the application, by email or through another appropriate channel, depending on available product capabilities and legal requirements.

8. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on actual use and the enabled release configuration, categories may include:

  • Account and eligibility: email, handle, username, DID, account creation and recovery records, age-related information.
  • Public activity: profile, posts, replies, pictures, videos, follows, likes, reposts, lists, searches, blocks, mutes and reports.
  • Technical and safety: IP address, device/app versions, login and security logs, CAPTCHA signals and notification tokens where enabled.
  • Communications: correspondence about support, safety, privacy, legal requests and account deletion.
  • Diagnostics: only when a production diagnostic tool is enabled and confirmed in the data inventory.

Do not infer Bluesky's direct-message, payment, precise-location or contact-upload practices for Unusu@ls. Each data type requires exact-build evidence before publication.

9. Cookies

Any essential session, network-security or browser-storage mechanisms used by our websites or related app services must be inventoried. The publicly deployed corporate website's own notice describes its controls separately.

Analytics, advertising or preference cookies must not be claimed as active or absent without an actual live-host and SDK audit. The final policy will disclose verified cookie purposes and choices.

10. How We Use Personal Data

Potential purposes include account creation and authentication, delivery of feeds and content, account recovery, notifications where enabled, spam prevention, fraud detection, moderation, issue resolution, service security and legal compliance.

Each purpose must be paired with the applicable Nigerian and any relevant international legal basis, rather than inheriting Bluesky's jurisdiction-specific rationale.

11. How We Share your Personal Data

Public AT Protocol records may be distributed to third-party servers, indexing services and other applications. We may also engage service providers for hosting, network security, email forwarding and anti-abuse functions, subject to verified vendor and contractual arrangements.

Our present planning records mention Unusuals-operated PDS/media infrastructure, Cloudflare, Gmail and hCaptcha. This list must be reconciled with actual production configuration before publication.

12. International data transfers

AT Protocol federation and external service providers may process information outside Nigeria. We will identify relevant locations and assess transfer safeguards and lawful bases. Any categorical promise of local-only storage or a particular international transfer mechanism is on hold pending vendor and data-flow verification.

13. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, portability and withdrawal of consent where relevant. Send a request to privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng and provide enough information for proportionate verification; never send a password or private key.

Independent federated copies may be beyond our direct control. You may also raise concerns with an appropriate data-protection regulator, including the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where applicable.

14. Retention of Personal Data

The existing Unusu@ls V1 candidate suggests retaining security logs and backups normally for up to 90 days and certain support, safety and deletion evidence normally for up to 24 months, subject to necessity and legal exceptions. These are proposed schedules, not audited production guarantees.

Final periods must be backed by actual database, storage, backup, moderation and email workflows. See Account Deletion for user request instructions and federation limitations.

15. Supplemental Notice for Certain Jurisdictions

Bluesky's reference policy includes additional explanations for particular jurisdictions. Unusu@ls will only adopt a supplemental notice after determining whether it offers service to people protected by those laws and verifying the applicable controller duties, lawful bases, representatives, cross-border transfers and available user-rights processes.

Review hold: do not present US state, EU/UK or Brazil-specific rights and officers as current Unusu@ls arrangements without legal verification.

16. Security

Security controls are intended to include restricted access, secure communications, credential protection, audit evidence, backups and incident response. No organisation can promise absolute protection. Specific statements about encryption, retention, recovery, access or monitoring require infrastructure evidence.

17. Children's Personal Information

Unusu@ls is not intended for children under 13 or below any higher applicable minimum age. Child protection and age screening are important operational gates; the exact release's implementation must be verified. Report suspected child exploitation or grooming via safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng, sending links and descriptions rather than illegal media.

18. Contact Us

Unusuals Network is responsible for this product's data-handling disclosures. Contact privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng about privacy rights or data questions; support@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for account issues; safety@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for abuse; legal@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng for legal matters.
