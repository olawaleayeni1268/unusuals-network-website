Website Privacy

This notice applies to the Unusuals Network corporate website. Product-specific privacy information for Unusu@ls may be published separately.

1. Who we are

Unusuals Network is a Nigerian information and communication technology business. For privacy enquiries relating to this corporate website, email privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng.

2. Information this website processes

This V1 corporate website does not contain account registration, payment processing, advertising trackers or marketing forms. If you contact us by email, we process the information you choose to send so we can receive, review and respond to your message.

Our hosting and network providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps and security logs as necessary to deliver and protect the website.

3. Cookies and analytics

Unusuals Network does not intentionally deploy application-level analytics or marketing cookies on this V1 corporate website. Hosting or security infrastructure may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms to provide and protect the service.

4. Why information may be processed

Information may be processed to operate and secure the website, respond to correspondence, prevent abuse, maintain records, and meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Retention

Correspondence and technical records are retained only for as long as reasonably needed for their operational, security, legal or dispute-resolution purpose, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Your enquiries

To ask a privacy question or exercise an applicable data right, contact privacy@unusuals.worldofchampionsnetwork.com.ng.

This page covers the corporate website only. The Unusu@ls application has a broader product privacy policy that is subject to separate launch and publication controls.
